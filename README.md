# CFD-analysis-of-a-turbulent-flow
This report presents a computational fluid dynamics (CFD) analysis of two engineering flow problems: turbulent flow through a diffuser and external flow around an airfoil (NACA4415). The simulations were performed using OpenFOAM, investigating the effects of mesh refinement, turbulence modelling, and near wall treatment on the numerical results.

For the diffuser case, a grid independence study was conducted using three mesh resolutions. The results showed good agreement between the medium and fine meshes and correctly captured the expected flow physics, including velocity reduction, pressure recovery, and total pressure losses. Additional simulations performed without wall functions confirmed the accuracy and robustness of the numerical solution. In particular, a velocity reduction coefficient of approximately 0.84 was obtained, confirming the presence of irreversible energy losses within the diffuser.

For the airfoil case, the aerodynamic behaviour was analyzed at different angles of attack using both the Spalart–Allmaras and k-$\varepsilon$ turbulence models. The comparison of lift and drag coefficients, pressure distributions, wall shear stress, and wake profiles demonstrated grid-independent and physically consistent results. A good agreement was observed between the different turbulence models and wall treatments, providing confidence in the reliability of the simulations.
